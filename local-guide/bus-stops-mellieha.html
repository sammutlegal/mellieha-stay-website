Transport

Bus stops in front make car-free days realistic.

The flat is useful even if you do not want to rent a car every day. Step outside, catch the bus, and keep simple days simple.

Guest tip

Use buses for simple local movement; choose car or taxi for tighter schedules and multi-stop days.

Why this matters.

Many Malta stays assume a car. This location gives guests more flexibility because the bus stops are in front of the flat. That means beach days, local movement, ferry connections, and relaxed evenings can be planned without making transport a daily project.

The best direct-booking version of this page should later include exact stop names, current route numbers, and links to official schedules.

You do not need every day to be a driving day.

Good bus-use cases.

  • Simple local trips around Mellieha and nearby areas.
  • Beach days when parking may be annoying.
  • Ferry connections when timing is relaxed.
  • Evenings where one person does not want to drive.

When to use a car instead.

If you want to combine several towns, beaches, or viewpoints in one day, a car or taxi can still be easier. The point is choice: the flat gives you both practical public transport and northern Malta road access.
