Island routes

Mellieha is a practical base for Gozo and Comino days.

The north of Malta makes island-hopping feel more natural. Ferry days, boat trips, Popeye Village, and coastal stops can all fit into the stay.

Best for

Guests who want beach days plus one or two bigger island routes.

Why base yourself here.

If Gozo, Comino, or the northern coast are part of the plan, Mellieha makes sense. You are not choosing the edge of Malta; you are choosing the side of Malta that makes those trips easier.

From the flat, guests can plan ferry days, boat trips, northern coast stops, and relaxed evenings without relocating during the holiday.

Stay in one place. Use the north well.

Good route ideas.

  • Gozo ferry day with a simple morning start.
  • Comino or Blue Lagoon boat trip, planned with seasonal timing.
  • Popeye Village plus northern coast stops.
  • Beach day followed by dinner walking distance from the flat.

What to confirm before going.

Ferry and boat timings vary by season and conditions. Before launch, this page should link to the relevant current operators and official information.
