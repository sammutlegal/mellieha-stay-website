Why this page exists.

Guests do not only search for apartments. They search for what is happening, what will be busy, what to book, and what to expect when they arrive. This page should stay practical and current.

July and August notes.

  • Start beach days earlier when possible.
  • Book popular restaurants ahead on weekends.
  • Expect busier roads and beaches at peak times.
  • Keep water and basics ready from the store in front.
Fresh local notes make the direct site more useful than a listing.

September note.

The Mellieha festa for the Nativity of Our Lady is celebrated on 8 September. This can be a strong page for guests visiting around late summer and early autumn.
